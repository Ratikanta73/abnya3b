about

Abinya is the annual project showcase of Siksha ’O’ Anusandhan University, where student-built projects meet industry. Edition 3.1 brings 30 teams to one floor: 20 showcase projects and 10 finalist teams from SOA TechXpress, the university’s 24-hour multidisciplinary hackathon.

Industry delegates, faculty and students walk the exhibition, question the builders and spot the ideas worth taking further. Two panel discussions bring practitioners on stage, and the day closes with the felicitation of Abinya 3.1 and SOA TechXpress winners, followed by a cultural night.
